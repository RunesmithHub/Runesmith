Runesmith works with [Forgejo](https://forgejo.org) servers, such as [Codeberg](https://codeberg.org), through the
[Gitea and Forgejo](./gitea.mdx) plugin, `runesmith.gitea`. That page covers signing in, **Settings › Forgejo**, registering an OAuth2
application and the `forgejo.oauthClientIds` setting.
