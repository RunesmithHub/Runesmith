---
title: SDKs
description: See the SDKs of your languages on your computer, pick the default of each kind, and download, update and remove them.
---

The **SDKs** page of the settings lists the SDKs that the plugins of your languages found on your computer or that Runesmith installed
for them. From there you pick the one builds and new projects use, download new ones from their vendors, update them and remove the ones
Runesmith installed. Runesmith knows no SDK by itself: each section of the page comes with the plugin of a language, and the page is
empty until you install one.

## Open the page

Choose **File › SDKs...**, or open **File › Settings** and pick **SDKs** in the list at the left. The page has a section per kind of SDK,
each with its SDKs newest first.

Each row shows the SDK's version, its vendor when the kind has several, its folder and processor, and where it came from:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Runesmith** | Runesmith downloaded and installed it. You can remove it from the page. |
| **System** | It was installed outside Runesmith, such as by a package manager or an installer. Runesmith never removes it. |

**Refresh** looks for SDKs again, such as after you installed one yourself.

## Choose the default

Select **Default** on a row to make that SDK the default of its kind. Until you choose, the newest one is the default. What the default
decides, such as which SDK builds your projects, is up to the plugin; its documentation says.

## Download an SDK

<Steps>

1. Click **Download...** in the section of the kind you want.
2. Pick what to install. A kind with one vendor asks for the **Channel** and the **Version**. A kind with several asks for the
   **Vendor**, the **Version**, the **Package** where the vendor has more than one, the **Architecture** and the **Release**. The dialog
   starts at the newest long-term support version and shows the size of the download.
3. Click **Install**. The dialog and the status bar show the download and the install; **Cancel** stops it.

</Steps>

Badges mark the releases: **LTS** for long-term support, **Preview** for versions that are not released yet, and **Latest** for the
newest version in the list.

Runesmith checks every download against the checksum its vendor publishes before it unpacks it, and installs nothing when they differ.
Where the releases of a kind come from is written under the dialog's title.

Installed SDKs go into the `sdks` folder of Runesmith's data folder: `$XDG_DATA_HOME/runesmith` (usually `~/.local/share/runesmith`) on
Linux, `~/Library/Application Support/Runesmith` on macOS and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Runesmith` on Windows, or `data` inside `RUNESMITH_HOME`
when you set it.

Runesmith reaches the vendors' services only while the SDKs page or the download dialog is open, and keeps their lists for a day.

## Update an SDK

When a newer version exists, the row shows **Update to** with that version, a newer one in the same release line. The update installs
next to the SDK you have, which stays until you remove it.

## Remove an SDK

Click **Remove** (the trash icon) on a row with the **Runesmith** badge and confirm.

## NixOS

The SDKs Runesmith downloads are built for other Linux distributions. On NixOS they run through `nix-ld`; turn it on with
`programs.nix-ld.enable = true;` in your system configuration. Without it, starting a program from Runesmith's SDK folder fails with an
error about a missing program loader.
