---
title: References
description: Find every use of a symbol with Find References, go to its implementations, and see its other uses highlighted in the file.
---

Find References lists every place a symbol is used, and Go to Implementation finds the implementations of an interface or an abstract
member. Both put what they find in the **References** panel, by file, with each line. While the caret rests on a symbol, its other uses in
the file are highlighted.

## Find references

<Steps>

1. Put the caret on a name and press <Keys>Shift+F12</Keys>, or right-click it and choose **Find References**.
2. When the symbol is used in one place only, Runesmith goes there. Otherwise the **References** panel opens with every use, the symbol's
   declaration included, grouped by file.
3. Move through the list with <Keys>Up</Keys> and <Keys>Down</Keys>: the editor shows each place as you go, and the list keeps the focus.
   Press <Keys>Enter</Keys>, or click a row, to go to it. **Go › Back** returns to where you were.

</Steps>

The panel's title says what was searched for and how many places it found in how many files. Each row shows the line number and the line,
with the name in bold. In the list, <Keys>F4</Keys> selects the next place and <Keys>Shift+F4</Keys> the previous one. A new search
replaces the list.

## Go to implementation

Press <Keys>Ctrl+F12</Keys>, or right-click and choose **Go to Implementation**, on an interface, an abstract or virtual member, or a
method of an interface. One implementation opens at once; several are listed in the **References** panel. When the language finds none,
a short message beside the caret says so.

## Highlighted occurrences

A moment after the caret stops on a name, the language highlights the other uses of the same symbol in the file, with a faint background:
places that assign or declare the symbol in blue, places that read it in grey. Each is also marked beside the vertical scroll bar. The
highlights go away when you select text or move the caret off them.

## Where the results come from

References, implementations and highlighted occurrences come from the language's server and from plugins. Without either, Find References
says that it found nothing. The [Editor extensions](/plugins/language-features#references-and-implementations) page shows how a plugin adds
them.
