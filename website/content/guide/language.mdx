---
title: Language
description: Show Runesmith in another language with a language pack, choose what partly translated plugins show, and see how much of each plugin is translated.
---

Runesmith shows English, and any language a language pack from the [plugin hub](./plugins.mdx) adds. Plugins are translated by their
authors or by language packs; text that no translation has shows in English. A change of language shows at once, without a restart.

## Choose a language

<Steps>

1. Install the language pack of your language: open **Plugins**, choose **Browse** and look for it, or follow the suggestion Runesmith
   shows when your system's language has an official pack.
2. Open **Settings** (<Keys>Ctrl+,</Keys>) at **Appearance**.
3. Pick the language under **Language**. Each language shows how much of Runesmith it translates, such as **Nederlands (Dutch), 94%**.

</Steps>

**Follow the system** shows your system's language when an installed pack covers it, and English otherwise. It is the default, so after
installing the pack of your system's language you need not choose anything. A regional language uses the pack of its base language: Dutch
for Belgian Dutch (`nl-BE`), for example. Dates and numbers follow the language too.

Runesmith suggests the official pack of your system's language once, when it is not installed. **Don't suggest again** stops the
suggestion for good.

## Plugins in your language

The plugin manager shows how much of each plugin your language translates, beside its name: **Nederlands, 62%** for a partly translated
plugin, **English only** for one without a translation. A language pack's entry lists what it translates, such as
**Nederlands (Dutch): Runesmith 94%, Git 100%**.

A plugin whose translation is incomplete shows its translated text in your language and the rest in English. To show such a plugin entirely
in English instead, set **Partly translated plugins** to **Show in English** in Settings, under **Language**. **Minimum translation** sets how
much a plugin must translate to show in your language then; the default is 80 percent.

## Languages written from right to left

In a language written from right to left, such as Arabic or Hebrew, the window's layout runs from right to left too: the side panels,
menus and lists swap sides. The editor keeps code left to right.

## Settings

| Setting | Key | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Language** | `appearance.language` | `system`, or a language tag such as `en` or `nl` |
| **Partly translated plugins** | `localization.untranslatedPlugins` | `mix`; `english` shows a plugin below the minimum in English |
| **Minimum translation** | `localization.minimumCoverage` | `80` percent |
| **Pseudo-localization** | `localization.pseudo` | Off. For developers: shows every translatable text accented, longer and in brackets |

These settings apply to you, not to a folder: a folder's settings file cannot change them.
