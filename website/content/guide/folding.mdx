---
title: Folding
description: Fold blocks of code, comments and imports away, with the gutter, the keyboard or the View menu.
---

Folding hides the lines of a block, such as a method's body, so the code around it fits on screen. A folded block keeps its first line and
shows a placeholder after it. The language says which lines fold; for a language that does not, the editor folds by indentation.

## Fold and unfold

Move the pointer over the gutter: a chevron shows beside each line that starts a block. Click it to fold the block, and click the chevron
or the placeholder at the end of the line to unfold it. A folded block keeps its chevron, pointing right.

| Command | Keys | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fold** | <Keys>Ctrl+Shift+[</Keys> | Folds the innermost block around the caret. |
| **Unfold** | <Keys>Ctrl+Shift+]</Keys> | Unfolds the folded block on the caret's line. |
| **Fold All** | <Keys>Ctrl+Alt+[</Keys> | Folds every block of the file. |
| **Unfold All** | <Keys>Ctrl+Alt+]</Keys> | Unfolds every block. |

The commands are also in the **View** menu.

## Folded lines

- The caret moves over folded lines: <Keys>Up</Keys> and <Keys>Down</Keys> skip them, and so does <Keys>Right</Keys> at the end of a folded
  line.
- Going to a place inside a folded block, such as a search result, a problem or a definition, unfolds it.
- Folded blocks stay folded as you type. A block unfolds when an edit changes its hidden lines, such as an undo or a replace.
- Copying a selection that spans a folded block copies its hidden lines too.

## What folds

The language decides what folds: blocks of code, comments of several lines, runs of imports, and regions marked in the code, such as with
`#region`. A region can show its own text in place of the placeholder. Without a language that says, a line folds the lines after it that
are indented deeper. The [Editor extensions](/plugins/language-features#folding) page shows how a plugin adds folding.
